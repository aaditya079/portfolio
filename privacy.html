3. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Voluntary Inquiries (Contact Form & Direct Email)

When you send a message through our contact form or directly via email (aadityasrinivasan079@gmail.com), you provide your name, email address, and message contents. We use this information solely to respond to your professional or technical inquiry. The legal basis for processing is our legitimate interest in communicating with prospective collaborators and employers, or your explicit consent when submitting the contact form.

B. Client-Side Local Storage (Browser LocalStorage)

To provide a consistent experience, we store three functional values in your browser's localStorage:

  • reze-mode: Stores your visual theme preference (cafe or bomb).
  • reze-volume: Stores your selected volume level for background ambient audio (between 0.0 and 1.0).
  • reze-cookie-consent: Stores your acknowledgment of our local storage policy.

These values remain strictly on your client device and are never sent to any server.

C. Third-Party Resource Delivery (CDNs)

To serve fonts and icons efficiently, this site loads assets from:

  • Google Fonts (Google LLC): Font stylesheets and files. Google may log your device's IP address for caching and security purposes.
  • Unpkg CDN (Cloudflare, Inc. / npm): Boxicons icon library. Your IP address may be processed for DDoS mitigation and asset delivery.
