Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026 · Last Updated: September 17, 2026

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and web storage technologies (such as HTML5 localStorage) are small text files or key-value entries placed on your computing device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites function efficiently and remember user preferences.

2. Does This Site Use Tracking Cookies?

No. This website does not set any third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, behavioral profiling trackers, or analytics tags. We do not use cookies to identify, track, or profile you across the web.

3. Exactly What Data Do We Store Locally?

We utilize standard browser localStorage strictly for functional interface preferences. These keys reside only inside your browser sandbox and are never transmitted to our servers:

Storage Key Purpose Type Duration
reze-mode Remembers your visual theme selection (Café Crossroads vs Bomb Devil mode). Strictly Necessary / Functional Persistent until cleared
reze-volume Remembers your volume slider setting for the ambient audio player. Functional Preference Persistent until cleared
reze-cookie-consent Records that you acknowledged this storage and privacy banner. Strictly Necessary Persistent until cleared

4. Third-Party Embeds & Content Delivery

When loading this website, your browser establishes requests to standard Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) to fetch typography and icon fonts:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): Google serves web fonts. No cookies are sent with font requests according to Google Fonts privacy documentation.
  • Boxicons via Unpkg: Provides vector interface icons via Cloudflare CDN.

5. How to Manage or Clear Local Storage

You have full control over your browser storage at all times. You can inspect, modify, or delete any values stored by this site through your browser's Developer Tools (F12 > Application / Storage > Local Storage), or by following these steps:

  • Google Chrome & Microsoft Edge: Go to Settings > Privacy and Security > Clear browsing data > Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data > Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Go to Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.

Clearing your local storage will simply reset your theme to Café Crossroads and reset the audio player volume to default (28%).
